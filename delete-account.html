Delete your YChat account

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Delete it in the app

  1. Open YChat and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Can't open the app?

Email ychat.support@gmail.com from the same Gmail address you use for YChat, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: username, display name, photo, bio, note and district
  • Your friends, friend requests and friend chat messages
  • Your settings, birth year and notification token

What we may keep

  • Reports you made or that were made about you, and the moderation records linked to them, as long as needed for safety and legal reasons.
  • If your account was banned: a scrambled (hashed) identifier so you can't sign up again. It can't be turned back into your email.
  • IP address records, which expire automatically within 30 days.

See our Privacy Policy for more details.
