Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 24 October 2026

YChat has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, and any sexual contact with or about minors.

Our standards

  • Adults only. YChat is for people aged 18 or older. Users must enter their date of birth, and anyone under 18 is refused. Accounts found to belong to minors are removed.
  • Prohibited. Any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits or endangers a child is strictly prohibited, as stated in our Terms of Service and Community Rules.
  • Safety by design. Strangers only see a small blurred profile photo, users can block anyone, and screenshots are restricted in chats.

How to report

  • In the app: tap Report in any chat or profile and choose the child-safety reason. These reports are marked urgent and are reviewed first.
  • By email: ychat.support@gmail.com

What we do

  • Child-safety reports trigger an immediate alert to our moderators.
  • We remove the content and permanently ban the accounts involved. Banned people are blocked from signing up again with the same Google account.
  • We preserve the evidence and report it to the relevant authorities, including the Sri Lanka Police and the National Child Protection Authority (NCPA), as required by law.
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.

Get help now

  • National Child Protection Authority (NCPA) child helpline: 1929
  • Police emergency: 119

Child safety contact

Our designated contact for child safety matters: ychat.support@gmail.com
