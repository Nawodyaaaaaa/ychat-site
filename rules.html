Community Rules

Last updated: 24 October 2026

YChat is for meeting new people and having good conversations. Keep it friendly and safe for everyone. Breaking these rules can get you warned, suspended or permanently banned.

1. 18+ only

You must be 18 or older. If someone tells you they are under 18, end the chat and report them.

2. Zero tolerance for child abuse

Any sexual content involving minors, or any attempt to groom, exploit or contact a child, means an instant permanent ban and a report to the authorities. See our Child Safety Standards.

3. No sexual content without consent

Don't send sexual messages or content to people who haven't clearly agreed. Never share intimate images of anyone without their consent.

4. No harassment, threats or hate

Don't insult, bully, threaten or stalk anyone. No hate based on race, ethnicity, religion, caste, gender, sexuality or disability.

5. Protect yourself and others

Don't share phone numbers, social media accounts, addresses or links with strangers in random chat. Never ask for or send money, OTP codes, passwords or bank details.

6. No scams, spam or selling

No advertising, selling, begging, fake giveaways or promoting other apps and channels.

7. Be yourself

Don't pretend to be someone else or use YChat to deceive people.

8. Nothing illegal

Don't use YChat for drugs, weapons, violence or any other illegal activity.

Something wrong? Tap Report or Block in the chat. Reports are reviewed by real people. For urgent danger, call the Police on 119.
