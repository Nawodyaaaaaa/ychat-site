Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information the YChat app ("YChat", "we", "us") collects, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. YChat is published by Y company, an independent developer based in Sri Lanka. We follow Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022.

Short version: we collect only what we need to run the app and keep people safe. We never sell your data. Random chats stay in server memory for about 5 minutes and are saved only if someone reports the chat. You can delete your account at any time.

1. Who can use YChat

YChat is only for adults aged 18 or older. We ask for your date of birth when you create a profile and refuse anyone under 18. If we learn that a user is under 18, we delete their account.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect your contacts, your precise GPS location, or your phone number.

3. Who can see your information

  • Strangers in random chat see your display name and a small blurred version of your photo. They do not see your username, user ID or district.
  • Friends see your profile as allowed by your settings in the Privacy Center.
  • YChat moderators can see reports, the reported chats and account details when they review abuse. Every moderator action is logged.

4. Services we use

We use these companies to run YChat. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage
  • Railway: our chat server (hosted in Singapore)
  • Google: Google Sign-In, Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications, and Google Play for app delivery and payments

Because these services run outside Sri Lanka, your data may be stored or processed in other countries.

5. Security

All data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to the database is restricted, and admin access requires two-step verification. Messages are encrypted in transit but are not end-to-end encrypted, so please don't share passwords, bank details or other sensitive information in chats. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile and friend chats: until you delete your account.
  • Random chats: about 5 minutes in memory, unless reported.
  • IP address records: up to 30 days.
  • Reports and moderation records: as long as needed to keep YChat safe, handle appeals, and meet legal obligations.
  • Banned accounts: we keep a scrambled (hashed) identifier so a banned person can't simply sign up again. It can't be turned back into your email.

7. Your rights and choices

You can:

  • View and edit your profile, and control what friends see in the Privacy Center.
  • Block or report anyone.
  • Turn notifications on or off.
  • Delete your account at any time. See how to delete your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing ychat.support@gmail.com from the Gmail you use for YChat.

8. Advertising and payments

YChat does not sell your personal data. If we add ads or paid features in the future, payments will be handled by Google Play, and we will update this policy before any change takes effect.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app before they apply. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: ychat.support@gmail.com
